<constraints>
- **Use shared rules, never reimplement them:** `validateFleetPlacement` (discriminated-union result with first-failure reason), `generateRandomFleet`, `cellsForPlacement`, `FLEET`, `SHIP_LENGTHS`, `GRID_SIZE`, `coordToLabel`. Coordinates are 0-based internally; classic labels (A1–J10) appear only via `coordToLabel` at the render boundary. Touching ships are legal (classic rules) — do not add adjacency restrictions.
- Draft fleet layout is local component/screen state — the ONLY placement data that ever reaches the store/server is the final `placeFleet` emit. The snapshot's `you.fleet` becomes non-null once the server accepts it.
- HTML5 drag-and-drop is fiddly across browsers; prefer pointer-event-based dragging (pointerdown on a ship → pointermove tracking → pointerup on a cell) or click-to-select + click-to-place with drag as enhancement. Pick one mechanism and make it solid rather than layering both.
- 90s placement timer and auto-place are Phase 6 — do NOT build timers here.
- This plan exclusively owns PlacementScreen.tsx; do not touch App.tsx, store.ts, or battle files (03-03 runs in parallel).
</constraints>
