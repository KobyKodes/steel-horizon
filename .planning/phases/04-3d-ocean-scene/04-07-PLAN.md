<task type="checkpoint:decision" gate="blocking">
  <decision>Choose the 3D ship-placement interaction model</decision>
  <context>
The 2D `PlacementBoard` (Phase 3) uses continuous pointer-drag: pointerdown lifts a ship, window-level pointermove tracks a ghost preview, pointerup drops it, R/right-click rotates. That pattern was chosen specifically to avoid HTML5 drag-and-drop fiddliness on a flat 2D grid. In a 3D perspective scene, continuous drag requires raycasting against the grid plane on every pointermove to find the hovered cell (more expensive and more failure-prone than 2D's simple rect-based `cellFromPoint`), and rotation "mid-drag" is less intuitive when the ship is a 3D model over a grid you can't perfectly foreshorten by eye.
  </context>
  <options>
    <option id="continuous-drag">
      <name>Continuous 3D drag (mirror the 2D pattern)</name>
      <pros>Familiar interaction carried over from Phase 3; feels the most "grab and place" cinematic</pros>
      <cons>Requires per-frame raycasting against HoloGrid during drag (reuse the R3F pointer-event system already built in 04-03, not a manual THREE.Raycaster); rotate-mid-drag is fiddlier in 3D perspective than top-down 2D</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="select-then-place">
      <name>Select ship, then click cell to place (with a separate rotate control)</name>
      <pros>Reuses `HoloGrid`'s existing click-to-select interaction from 04-03/04-05 almost as-is (no new drag machinery); simpler, more robust, easier to make accessible</pros>
      <cons>Less "grab and drag" tactile feel than continuous dragging; needs a small UI affordance for "currently selected ship to place" and a rotate toggle</cons>
    </option>
  </options>
  <resume-signal>Select: continuous-drag or select-then-place</resume-signal>
</task>
