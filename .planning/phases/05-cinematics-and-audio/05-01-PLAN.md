<objective>
Extend `CameraRig` with a dynamic shell-follow camera state and a hand-rolled screen-shake offset, without breaking the existing damped fleet/targeting view transitions.

Purpose: The firing sequence (05-03) needs the camera to chase a moving shell in flight, then shake on impact. 05-RESEARCH.md found drei's `<CameraShake>` component is incompatible as a sibling mount — it captures `camera.rotation` once and overwrites it every frame from that stale snapshot, fighting `CameraRig`'s own per-frame `camera.lookAt()` call. Shake must be computed inside `CameraRig`'s own `useFrame`, applied after `lookAt()`.
Output: `CameraRig` accepts an optional live-updating focus target for shell-follow, plus an imperative shake trigger, while `fleet`/`targeting` behavior is unchanged.
</objective>
